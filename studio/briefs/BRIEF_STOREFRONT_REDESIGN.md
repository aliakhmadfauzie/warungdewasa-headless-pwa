# 📋 Brief: Storefront Human UI 7-Step Sequence Redesign

> **SAVED DECISION RECORD**  
> *Project: Warung Dewasa Headless E-Commerce PWA*  
> *Status: Approved & Implemented*

---

## 1. Context & Goal
- **Deliverable Type**: Storefront Landing Page & Component Hierarchy
- **Business Objective**: Eliminate flat AI-generated look and boost COD checkout conversion by reintroducing deliberate human design choices.
- **After-State**: The visitor feels instant relief: their privacy is 100% safeguarded, parcel packaging is discreet, delivery in Medan takes less than 90 mins, and there is no trace on bank or e-commerce records.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Positioning
- **Target Audience**: Adults residing in Kota Medan, Deli Serdang, and Binjai.
- **Key Pain Point**: Hesitation to buy adult products online due to public delivery couriers and traceable bank statements.

---

## 3. Narrative Architecture (Prompt 03)
1. **Audience Qualifier Strip**: `KHUSUS KOTA MEDAN & SEKITARNYA`
2. **Hero Section (Asymmetric)**: Left 7 cols copy, right 5 cols live courier readiness card.
3. **Why Warung Dewasa**: Direct comparison (Plain box vs marketplace plastic resi; cash on delivery vs bank history).
4. **Winner Spotlight**: Dedicated hero feature for #1 recommended SKU.
5. **Catalog Grid**: Progressive batch reveal with 48px touch targets.
6. **Closing Reassurance**: 3 plain steps describing day-to-day experience + final ask.

---

## 4. Design Signatures
- Kicker tags: `kicker-badge` with pulsing emerald dot.
- Cards: `signature-card` with double-layer border and soft emerald hover glow.
- Divider: `signature-divider` smooth horizontal gradient.
