# 📖 Warung Dewasa — Official Brand Guidelines

> **CONFIDENTIAL & INTERNAL DESIGN SYSTEM SPECIFICATION**  
> *Material 3 Emerald Design System for Web PWA and Native Android*

---

## 1. Brand Core & Persona

### Core Value Proposition
> **"Privasi 100% Terjaga, Pesan Langsung Bayar COD di Medan."**

- **Tone of Voice**: Diskrit, Tenang, Lugas, Reassuring (memberikan rasa aman dan lega, bukan sensasional atau vulgar).
- **Target Audience**: Konsumen dewasa di Kota Medan dan sekitarnya yang menginginkan akses produk keintiman & kesehatan resmi tanpa rasa cemas privasi terbongkar kepada kurir umum atau terekam di riwayat marketplace.
- **The "After-State"**: Rasa lega, bebas rasa malu, kemasan rapi tidak tembus pandang, dan transaksi beres saat barang di tangan.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

- **Display & Headings**: `Outfit` font (Clean, geometric, premium).
  - Main Hero Display: `text-3xl sm:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-extrabold tracking-tight`
  - Section Titles: `text-xl sm:text-3xl font-black text-white`
  - Price Tags: `font-display font-black text-emerald-400 tabular-nums text-base sm:text-lg`
- **Body & Inputs**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` font (High legibility, modern UI).
  - Body Text: `text-xs sm:text-sm text-slate-300 leading-relaxed font-normal`
  - Supporting Captions: `text-[11px] sm:text-xs text-slate-400`
- **Signature Kickers**:
  - Uppercase pill: `text-[10px] sm:text-xs font-black uppercase tracking-widest text-emerald-400`

---

## 4. Packaging & Courier Dispatch Protocol

All orders must adhere to the **Zero-Leakage Packaging Protocol**:
1. **Outer Box**: Plain unprinted brown corrugated box (`kardus polos cokelat`).
2. **Inner Cushioning**: Black/opaque bubble wrap around product container.
3. **Shipping Label**:
   - Sender: *Toko Aksesoris / WD Store Medan* (Never use words like *Dewasa*, *Kondom*, *Poppers*, *Seks*).
   - Item Description: *Aksesoris / Suplemen Kesehatan*.
4. **Courier Payment**: Tunai / COD di tempat langsung ke tangan pelanggan.
